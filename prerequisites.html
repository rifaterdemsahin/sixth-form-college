Building AI Agents for Real Business Growth: before you arrive

Please complete this checklist at least 2 days before the session. Set-up takes about 45 minutes. In class, every minute goes on building, not installing.

1Bring on the day

  • A laptop (Windows 10/11, macOS 12+ or Linux) that you can install software on. Tablets, Chromebooks and phones will not work. If your college laptop is locked down, tell us now (see section 5).
  • Your charger. The session runs for two hours and sockets are limited.
  • Your phone, for two-factor sign-in codes.
  • Your passwords for the accounts below. You will sign in to all of them on the day.

Under 18, or no bank card? Some providers (Fly.io, Stripe, model APIs) need you to be 18+ or to add a card. Don't use a parent's card without their permission. Create the accounts you can, and we will pair you with an instructor account for the rest. You will still build and deploy everything.
Never share secrets. API keys, passwords and recovery codes stay private. Don't post them on the whiteboard, in chat or in code you push to GitHub. In Module 4 we store them in a key vault.

4Check it all works (5 minutes)

Open a terminal (Terminal on macOS, PowerShell on Windows) and run each command. Each one should print a version number.

  • git --version
  • node --version Should be v22 or newer.
  • fly version and then fly auth login Opens your browser to sign in.
  • Sign in to GitHub, n8n, Fly.io and Stripe in Chrome and stay signed in.
  • Open the session whiteboard: canva.link/6ruoc3i38l9870d

5Think ahead

  • Bring one idea. Pick a task you repeat every week, the tools it touches and who should check the result. You will turn it into a bot.
  • Locked-down college laptop? If you can't install software, email the organiser before the session so we can arrange a loan laptop or a browser-only route.
  • Stuck on set-up? Arrive 15 minutes early. Marianna and the team will check laptops at the door.
